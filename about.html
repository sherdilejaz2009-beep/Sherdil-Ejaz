01 / MY STORY

More Than Just A Screen.

A little background about the person, the curiosity and the motivation behind the work.

My name is Sherdil Ejaz. I am interested in technology, web development and creative digital experiences. What started as curiosity about how websites are made has gradually become an important part of my learning journey.

I enjoy exploring the connection between design and development. A website should not only look good; it should also be easy to understand, comfortable to use and work properly across different devices.

I am building my knowledge step by step, practising with real website projects and improving my understanding of both frontend and backend development. Every project gives me an opportunity to learn something new and improve the way I approach problems.

I believe that becoming a good developer takes more than knowing programming languages. It requires patience, problem-solving, creativity, consistency and the willingness to keep learning.

"I don't just want to learn how to build websites. I want to understand how meaningful digital experiences are created."

05 / MY MINDSET

Good Design Gets Attention. Good Thinking Creates Value.

I want to approach every project with a clear purpose. Before thinking about effects and colors, I believe it is important to understand the problem, the users and what the website is expected to achieve.

My aim is to keep improving the balance between visual quality, usability, performance and functional development.

Think through problems before writing code
Keep layouts organised and easy to understand
Consider users across different screen sizes
Pay attention to speed and performance
Learn, test, improve and repeat
